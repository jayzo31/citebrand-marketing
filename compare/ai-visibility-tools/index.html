AI Visibility Tools Compared: Pricing, Engines and What Each One Does

Updated September 2026

The main tools for tracking and improving how your brand appears in ChatGPT, Perplexity and other AI answer engines are Peec AI, Otterly.ai, Profound, Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, Ahrefs Brand Radar, Scrunch AI, Writesonic GEO, Okara AI CMO and Hall. If you are looking for an alternative to Profound or Peec AI, the self-serve options with published prices start at free (Hall, 25 questions checked weekly) and $29/month (Otterly.ai Lite, 15 prompts). Profound dropped its self-serve plans in September 2026 and now offers a 7-day trial followed by custom Enterprise pricing, reported at $2k–5k+ per month.

For a small agency, entry plans as of September 2026 cost: Hall free, Otterly.ai $29, Writesonic GEO $79, Peec AI €95, Semrush $99 per domain, Okara AI CMO $129, Ahrefs Brand Radar $199 and Scrunch AI $250–300 per month. If you need a tool that shows what to fix rather than only a visibility score, look for action features: Writesonic GEO lists an Action Center and content agents, Okara AI CMO executes changes through GitHub pull requests and CMS publishing, and Profound lists Agents/Workflows. To find which websites AI chatbots cite about your competitors but never mention you, CiteBrand's source gap analysis finds the exact URLs where competitors are cited but you're not.

How to evaluate any AI visibility tool

  • Measure frequency, not rank. The same prompt returns the same brand list less than 1% of the time across runs, so a tool should report how often you appear across repeated samples (SparkToro).
  • Ask whether it checks the consumer app or the API. For ChatGPT, API answers and consumer-app answers share only about 24% of brands and 4% of sources (Surfer). Peec AI states that it scrapes real chat interfaces.
  • Don't use Google rankings as a proxy. Only about 12% of URLs cited by AI assistants rank in Google's top 10 for the same query (Ahrefs).
  • Check Bing, not only Google. About 87% of SearchGPT citations matched Bing's top 20 results (Seer Interactive).

What tends to change AI citations

  • Freshness. AI-cited content is about 26% fresher than organic results, and the median citation half-life is about 4.5 weeks (Ahrefs freshness study, via NoGood; Scrunch/Stacker citation half-life study). Keep a visible updated date and refresh key pages.
  • Third-party lists. Third-party "best of" lists win about 81% of listicle citations versus 19% for brand-owned lists (Ahrefs). Being included on independent lists counts, not only publishing your own.
  • Crawlable, server-rendered pages. AI search crawlers need to be allowed in robots.txt and able to read your text without running JavaScript.
  • llms.txt and JSON-LD are hygiene only. llms.txt showed no correlation with AI citations across about 300k domains, and Google states it does not use it (SE Ranking). JSON-LD schema showed no measurable AI-citation lift in a controlled study; keep it for classic rich results (Ahrefs).
