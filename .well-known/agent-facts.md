## What we do

CiteBrand is a diagnostic tool for AI search visibility. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "The diagnostic tool for AI search visibility. Stop guessing, start fixing.")

## Products

- AI visibility diagnostics: CiteBrand gives actionable diagnostics for visibility in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "Get actionable diagnostics for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity visibility.")
- Source Gap Analysis: Source Gap Analysis finds the exact URLs where competitors are cited but you are not. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "Source Gap Analysis Find the exact URLs where competitors are cited but you're not.")
- Sentiment Diagnostics: Sentiment Diagnostics shows why LLMs describe your pricing as expensive or your UX as complex. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "Sentiment Diagnostics Discover why LLMs think your pricing is "expensive" or UX is "complex".")
- Intent Mismatch Detection: Intent Mismatch Detection shows the content format that wins in AI answers, such as comparison tables instead of blog posts. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "AI wants comparison tables but you have blog posts? We show the content format that wins.")
- Knowledge Graph Check: Knowledge Graph Check checks whether you are in Wikidata, Google Knowledge Graph and other sources LLMs trust. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "Are you in Wikidata? Google Knowledge Graph? We check all sources LLMs trust.")
- Hallucination Detection: Hallucination Detection compares LLM answers against your uploaded truth files to find wrong pricing, features, or facts. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "Upload your truth files. We find every time an LLM gets your pricing, features, or facts wrong.")
- Competitor Tracking: Competitor Tracking shows where competitors appear in AI answers and you don't. (source: https://citebrand.ai/, "Competitor Tracking See exactly where competitors appear and you don't.")
