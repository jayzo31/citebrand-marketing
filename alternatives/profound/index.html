Frequently asked questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to Profound?

Yes. As of September 2026, Hall has a free tier (25 questions, checked weekly), Otterly.ai starts at $29/month (15 prompts), Writesonic GEO at $79/month, Peec AI at €95/month (50 prompts) and Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit at $99/month per domain. Profound's reported Enterprise range is $2k–5k+ per month.

Does Profound still have self-serve pricing?

No. Profound dropped its self-serve plans in September 2026. It now offers a 7-day trial, then custom Enterprise pricing (tryprofound.com/pricing).

Is Profound still $99 a month?

No. AI answers still quote "Profound from $99/mo", but as of September 2026 Profound's pricing page lists a 7-day trial followed by Enterprise pricing only.

Which Profound alternative covers the most AI engines?

Among tools with published prices, Ahrefs Brand Radar lists 8 engines, Scrunch AI 7 (including Meta AI) and Hall 6. Profound lists 9.

Which alternative checks the real ChatGPT app rather than the API?

Peec AI states that it scrapes real chat interfaces. This matters because for ChatGPT, API answers and consumer-app answers share only about 24% of brands and 4% of sources (Surfer).

Can I try CiteBrand before paying?
