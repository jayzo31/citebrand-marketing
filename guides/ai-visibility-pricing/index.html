What AI search visibility tools cost a small agency (September 2026)

Updated September 2026 · prices seen 25 September 2026

A small agency pays from $29 a month for 15 prompts on Otterly.AI Lite to $489 a month for 400 prompts on Otterly.AI Premium; Semrush charges $99 per domain per month, billed annually, for 25 prompts on one domain. Among the other published prices we saw on 25 September 2026, Ahrefs Brand Radar's AI Visibility Index starts at $199/month, HubSpot AEO costs $50/month after a free trial, and classic brand monitoring on Brand24 Pro costs $499/month ($399/month billed annually).

For five clients tracked on 20 prompts each (100 prompts), that works out to $189/month on Otterly.AI Standard or $495/month on Semrush (5 domains × $99). Peec AI, Profound, Scrunch AI, Writesonic GEO, Okara AI CMO and Hall did not show a price we could record in our 25 September 2026 check, so we link their pricing pages instead of quoting a figure.

This page is published by CiteBrand, which sells an AI visibility tool. Every competitor price below was read from the vendor's own pricing page on 25 September 2026 and is linked. Prices change; check the vendor page before you buy.

Worked example: 5 clients × 20 prompts

An agency with 5 clients that tracks 20 prompts per client needs 5 × 20 = 100 prompts a month.

  • Otterly.AI Standard: $189/month. Standard includes 100 search prompts, which covers all 100, and has unlimited workspaces, so each client can have its own (otterly.ai/pricing, seen 25 Sep 2026).
  • Semrush AI Visibility (Base): 5 domains × $99 = $495/month, billed annually. Semrush prices per domain, so each client domain needs its own $99 plan, and each includes 25 prompts, enough for 20 per client (semrush.com/pricing/ai, seen 25 Sep 2026).
